Privacy Policy for Kiters Messenger Last updated: 23 October 2026 Kiters Messenger ("we", "our", "the app") is a chat and social app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. 1. Information we collect Account information: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account when you sign in, plus any username or profile details you add. Content you share: messages, photos, videos, GIFs, reels, status updates, public chat posts, AI-generated images and links. Call data: when you make voice or video calls, audio and video are transmitted through our calling provider to connect the call. We do not record calls. Device information: device type, operating system, and a notification token to deliver messages and call alerts. 2. How we use your information To provide private chat, group chat, public chat, reels, status and calls To show message and incoming call notifications To generate AI images you request To keep the app secure and prevent abuse To improve app performance 3. Permissions Camera and microphone: for photos, videos and calls Storage / media: to upload and save photos and videos Notifications: to alert you about messages and calls Internet: to send and receive data We use these permissions only for the features described above. 4. Third-party services We use the following services to run the app: Google Sign-In and Firebase (Google): authentication, database, and notifications Cloudinary: storage and delivery of images and videos Cloudflare: processing of AI image generation requests Jaas (8x8 Jitsi as a Service): voice and video calls These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data. 5. Who can see your content Public chat posts and status are visible to other users of the app. Private and group messages are visible only to the people in that chat. 6. Data security We take reasonable steps to protect your data. However, no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. 7. Account and data deletion You can delete your account anytime from inside the app. When you delete your account, your profile and posts are removed from the app. You can also contact us at blimproducts@gmail.com to request deletion of your data. 8. Children's privacy Kiters Messenger is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. 9. User content and safety You must not post illegal, abusive or harmful content. We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate this. 10. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. 11.
